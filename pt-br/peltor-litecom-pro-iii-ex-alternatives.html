3M Peltor LiteCom Pro III Ex
Alternatives
Headsets ATEX comparados

The 3M Peltor LiteCom Pro III Ex is the go-to ATEX headset for teams that want built-in radio communication without carrying a separate device. But if your requirements differ — better speech clarity, lower cost, or Bluetooth-only — there are certified alternatives worth evaluating.

Por que olhar além do LiteCom Pro III Ex?

  • Você precisa de melhor voz em ruído alto. The LiteCom uses level-dependent attenuation. Sensear’s SENS Technology provides significantly better speech clarity in continuous 95+ dB environments.
  • Você já tem rádios bidirecionais. If your workers carry dedicated ATEX radios (Motorola, Hytera), the built-in PMR446 is redundant. A Sensear SM1P-Ex connects to your existing radio fleet.
  • Você precisa apenas de chamadas telefônicas. If workers pair headsets with ATEX phones and don’t use radios at all, the Peltor Alert XPI Ex is lighter and cheaper.
  • O orçamento é muito apertado. The wired ecom Ex-TRA 30 costs ~€150 vs ~€800 for the LiteCom. If simple voice is enough, the cost difference is significant.

1. Sensear SM1P-Ex

Melhor clareza de fala em ruído alto. Sensear’s patented SENS Technology isolates human speech from background noise, allowing face-to-face conversation even in 95+ dB environments without removing the headset. This is the SM1P-Ex’s decisive advantage over any Peltor headset. It connects to external two-way radios via cable and pairs via Bluetooth 5.0 with ATEX phones. Slightly more expensive (~€900 vs ~€800) but dramatically better for continuous high-noise environments.

2. Sensear SM1B-Ex

Tecnologia SENS a um preço mais baixo. The SM1B-Ex drops Bluetooth but keeps Sensear’s core speech enhancement. At ~€550, it’s cheaper than both the LiteCom Pro III Ex and the SM1P-Ex. Choose this if radio-only communication is sufficient and you don’t need wireless phone pairing.

3. 3M Peltor Alert XPI Ex

Proteção auditiva Bluetooth, sem rádio. If you’re paying for the LiteCom’s built-in radio but your workers never use it (because they have separate radios or only use phones), the Alert XPI Ex at ~€450 gives you Bluetooth 5.0 connectivity with Zone 1 certification and the highest NRR (29 dB) in this comparison. It lacks the built-in radio and speech enhancement, but for phone-only use cases, it’s the most cost-effective option.

4. ecom Ex-TRA 30

Com fio, zero manutenção. At ~€150, the ecom Ex-TRA 30 is the simplest ATEX audio option. No batteries to charge, no pairing to manage. Plugs directly into an ATEX phone or radio via 3.5mm jack. Obviously no noise cancellation or hearing protection — it’s a different product category. But for simple voice communication in lower-noise hazardous areas, it’s hard to beat on cost and reliability.

Onde o LiteCom Pro III Ex realmente vence

To be balanced: the LiteCom Pro III Ex is the only ATEX headset with a built-in license-free radio. For small teams that need simple push-to-talk communication without carrying additional equipment, nothing else in this list matches that convenience. If your use case is "headset + radio in one device, Zone 1 certified," the LiteCom has no direct competitor.
